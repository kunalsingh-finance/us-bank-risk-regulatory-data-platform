# Responsible Use

This project is an educational research and data-governance demonstration. It ranks eligible institutions relative to other banks in the same reporting quarter using public FDIC information. A high rank is not proof, a calibrated probability, or a regulatory determination.

Do not use it for official bank supervision, depositor decisions, bank-run speculation, trading recommendations, lending decisions, regulatory enforcement, public accusations against an institution, or claims that an institution will fail. Do not use synthetic demo institutions as if they were real.

Misinterpreted rankings can cause reputational harm, encourage unsound financial decisions, and obscure the difference between public accounting indicators and confidential supervisory evidence. Any legitimate research use should combine source verification, data-quality review, temporal context, competing explanations, uncertainty, and qualified professional judgment. The authors provide no regulatory, legal, investment, or financial advice.
