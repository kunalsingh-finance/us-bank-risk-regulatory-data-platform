# Alert Methodology

The primary monitoring rule is the frozen top 5% of eligible institutions in each reporting quarter. Secondary top-1% and top-10% flags support capacity analysis. Alert statuses are `No alert`, `Monitor`, `Elevated indicators`, and `Top-5% review`.

The 2024 Q4 prepared population contains 4,345 institutions: 44 in the top 1%, 218 in the top 5%, and 435 in the top 10%. The latest watchlist contains the 218 top-5% rows.

An alert means relative public-data indicators warrant analyst review. It is not a prediction that an institution will experience a specific outcome and must not trigger automatic adverse action.
